Household scrap

If you think Utah metal recycling for homeowners is limited to aluminum cans, think again. Bring the cans. Also bring the unused metal gathering dust in the house, attic, basement, or shed.

What non-ferrous means

Non-ferrous metals are materials whose value does not come principally from iron. Utah Metal Works buys non-ferrous metals such as:

  • Aluminum
  • Copper
  • Lead
  • Nickel and stainless alloys
  • Precious metals
  • Tin
  • Titanium
  • Zinc

See current prices per pound, or call if you are not sure what you have.

Household items you can recycle

  • Brass fixtures and brass knick-knacks
  • Aluminum foil
  • Aluminum siding, including gutters and downspouts
  • Christmas lights
  • Insulated and bare aluminum and copper wire
  • Screen doors and aluminum window frames
  • Cast aluminum barbecues
  • Plumbing pipe, valves, and connections
  • Aluminum pots and pans
  • Computer motherboards, keyboards, and hard drives (no monitors)
  • Oil-free car parts
  • Car batteries

If you do not know what metal an item is, call and we will tell you. Pre-sorted, clean material with nothing else attached pays better.

As easy as 1-2-3

  1. 1

    Drop it off

    805 Everett Avenue, just off Beck Street near 1500 North, during business hours. We do not pick up residential loads.

  2. 2

    Show ID and weigh

    Bring a state or federal ID (not required for aluminum cans only). We identify the metal and weigh it on a state-certified scale.

  3. 3

    Get paid

    Cash or check, on the spot.

Call or text the yard

No form. A person answers during business hours, and texts are welcome anytime.
