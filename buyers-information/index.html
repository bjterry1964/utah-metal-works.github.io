Services

Buyers information

This is the same buying program as our industrial service. If you sell scrap from a shop, a job site, or a plant, start here.

At Utah Metal Works, recycling industrial-related scrap metal is one of the things we do best. You simply will not find more knowledgeable, capable partners for your scrap handling and recycling efforts anywhere. We are known in the industry for our honesty and integrity, and you can be sure you are always getting a competitive return along with superior service.

We make it easy for you to cash in on non-ferrous metals from:

Industrial and manufacturing

  • Punchings
  • Skeleton plate
  • Solids of any metal

Machine shop and fabricator

  • Turnings and borings
  • Solids of any size or type

Aircraft and high-temperature alloys

  • Stainless, titanium, Inconel, Hastelloy, Monel, cupronickel
  • Metal analysis on site
  • Mixed or contaminated items are accepted and sorted

Electrician and plumber scrap

  • Insulated copper wire, bare wire, and bus bar
  • Copper pipe and tubing
  • Brass fixture valves
  • Heat exchangers and air-conditioning coils
  • Air conditioners and condenser coils
  • Electric motors

Demolition and construction

  • Clean or contaminated scrap
  • Segregated or mixed loads
  • Bins for job sites when you need them

How it works

Your business has other work to do. Here is the scrap side, kept simple.

  • We pick up most loads (minimum quantities and distance apply). You can also drop material at the Salt Lake City yard during business hours.
  • We provide bins, boxes, roll-off containers, and trailers.
  • Pickups can be on a schedule or only when you call.
  • We pick up within 48 hours, weather permitting.
  • You choose how we pay: weekly, monthly, or by the load.

Call or text and we will tell you what a load like yours is worth.
